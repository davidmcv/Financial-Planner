Market stress test (Monte Carlo)

—

Replays your household plan against ~100 years of (approximate) real annual returns for a UK/global equity portfolio, resampled in random multi-year blocks, to ask: how often does the money last? Withdrawals each year = your spending target minus guaranteed income (State + DB). Historical patterns are no guarantee of the future.

Blank = use living-standard target above
